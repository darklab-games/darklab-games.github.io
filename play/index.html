Training runs as wave 1 — flying, dashing, loot, combos. The real run picks up from wave 2 either way.
CONTROLS — PICK EITHER, SWITCH ANY TIME
MOUSERelaxed:  the ship flies to your cursor and your guns lock onto the nearest enemy automatically
W A S D+ MOUSEPrecise:  fly with the keys, aim wherever you point. Full control of where the shots go
LEFT CLICKDash — brief invincibility, dodge straight through bullets
SPACEFree / lock the cursor.  A run holds your mouse inside the window so it can't slide onto another screen mid-fight. Tap SPACE to hand it back, tap SPACE again (or click the game) to lock it in again — the game keeps running either way
1 2 3 4Q EWHEELswitch weapons — you start with LASER and unlock the rest from upgrades
ESCpause  ·  P pause  ·  M mute  ·  F fullscreen  ·  R reroll upgrades  ·  1-3 pick a card  ·  ENTER start / retry

THE RUN
▸ Clear a wave, pick ONE UPGRADE from three. 1-3 picks, R rerolls.
▸ HULL ♥ is your life. SHIELD ⬡ soaks hits first — it does NOT regrow on its own, refill it with ⬡ drops or the SIPHON upgrade.
▸ Wrecks drop loot: ★ score · ⬡ shield · ♥ hull · ⚡ overdrive · ⏳ time warp · 🧲 magnet · ☢ screen nuke.
▸ Every 5th wave is a BOSS. Learn the tells — red lines mean a shot is coming.
SURVIVAL TIPS
▸ Chain kills fast to build a COMBO — up to 10× score and it powers your streak bonuses.
▸ Dash has i-frames. Dash through bullet walls, not around them.
▸ Pick upgrades that stack. Three levels of one beats one level of three.
CONTROLS — TWO THUMBS, NO FIRE BUTTON
◀ LEFTSteer.  Press anywhere on the left half of the screen — a stick appears under your thumb, wherever you put it. Drag to fly, lift off to stop. There is no fixed pad to hunt for
DASHDodge.  The big button under your right thumb. Short, fast, and invincible while it lasts — go straight through bullets, not around them. It dims while it recharges
WPNthe small button above DASH cycles your weapons. You start with LASER and unlock the rest from upgrades
AUTONever aim, never shoot.  Your guns lock onto the nearest enemy and fire on their own. All of the skill in this game is where you fly
TAPbetween waves, tap a card to take that upgrade — or ↻ REROLL for a fresh three
⏸ 🔊top right corner: pause and mute. BACK pauses a run; from the menu, press BACK twice to exit
THE RUN
▸ Clear a wave, then take ONE UPGRADE out of the three you are dealt.
▸ HULL ♥ (top right) is your life. SHIELD ⬡ soaks hits first — it does NOT regrow on its own, refill it with ⬡ drops or the SIPHON upgrade.

PHONE TIPS
▸ Keep the thumb moving. Standing still is what ends runs — the swarm converges on wherever you stop.
▸ Steer from the far left edge so your hand never sits on top of the fight.
▸ Chain kills back to back for a COMBO — up to 10× score, and it feeds your streak bonuses.
▸ Keep a dash in hand. A charge you are holding is an escape; a charge on cooldown is a hull segment.
▸ Stack upgrades. Three levels of one beats one level of three.
