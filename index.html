The run

Clear a wave, pick one upgrade from three

Do that forty times and the starter laser has become a chain-lightning storm with homing rockets, orbiting drones and frag rounds that clear half the screen.

There is no shoot button. Your weapons lock the nearest target on their own, so every decision you make is about movement — read the field, thread the bullets, and dash straight through the wall instead of around it.

  • Auto-aim, manual survival

    The guns handle targeting. You handle staying alive, which turns out to be the hard part.

  • Four bosses, every fifth wave

    The Maw, Twin Lance, Hive Queen and the Void Sovereign. Every attack telegraphs — the red lines are your only warning.

  • Upgrades that stack

    Common, rare and epic. Three levels of one always beats one level of three.

  • A dash with real i-frames

    Brief invincibility, up to three charges. An offensive tool, not a panic button.
