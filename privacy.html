Void Strike · Android & Web

Privacy Policy

Last updated 9 August 2026

The short version. Void Strike stores nothing about you and sends nothing anywhere. The only data that leaves your device comes from Google AdMob, which serves the ads that keep the game free.

Void Strike ("the game") is published by DarkLab Games ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the game collects, what our advertising partner collects, and what you can do about it. It applies to the Android app and to the browser version on this site.

What we collect

Nothing. The game has no account system, no analytics, no crash reporting and no server of its own. We do not know who you are or that you played.

Your best score, best wave, total kills, sound preference and screen-shake setting are stored only on your device and are never transmitted. Uninstalling the app, or clearing your browser data, deletes them permanently. There is no copy anywhere else.

What our advertising partner collects

The game shows ads through Google AdMob. To select and measure ads, AdMob may collect and process:

  • your device's Advertising ID, a resettable identifier;
  • device information — model, operating system version, language and general region;
  • coarse location inferred from your IP address, at country or region level, never a precise position;
  • ad interaction data — which ads were shown, viewed or tapped.

This data goes directly to Google; we never receive it. It is used to choose ads, limit how often you see the same one, measure performance and detect fraud. Google's handling of it is governed by their policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data from apps that use its services

Where ads appear

Ads never interrupt a run. There are exactly three places one can appear:

  • Between runs — a full-screen ad may play when you tap Play Again, and never in your first two runs of a session.
  • Revive — an optional video you choose to watch to continue a run after dying.
  • Extra reroll — an optional video you choose to watch for another set of upgrade cards.

The last two only ever play if you tap the button yourself.

Your choices

Consent

In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland, the game shows Google's consent form on first launch and asks whether you accept personalised advertising. You can change that answer at any time from Pause → Ad Privacy Options.

Advertising ID

You can reset or delete your Advertising ID at any time in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting it stops ads being personalised. The game keeps working either way.

Turning ads off

Ads cannot be disabled inside the game, because they are what makes it free. Playing in airplane mode stops ads loading, and the game remains fully playable offline.

Children

Void Strike is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The Google Play listing declares a target audience of 13 and over. If you believe a child has provided personal data through the game, write to us and we will act on it.

Data retention, sharing and security

We hold no user data on any server, so there is nothing for us to retain, disclose, sell or lose. We do not sell data, because we have none. Retention of advertising data is governed by Google's policies linked above.
