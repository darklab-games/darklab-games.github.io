Common questions

There is no shoot button. Is that a bug?

No — that is the game. Your weapons target the nearest enemy on their own, so all your attention goes on movement. Run the training wave from the main menu if you want a ninety-second walkthrough.

My shield does not come back

Shields do not regenerate. Refill them with the blue ⬡ pickups that drop from wrecks, or take the Siphon upgrade, which rebuilds a segment every few kills. That is deliberate: it is what makes taking a hit matter.

The revive button did not appear

Revive is offered once per run, and only when an ad has finished loading. On a weak connection there may not be one ready. It never appears twice in the same run.

I watched an ad and got nothing

The reward only pays out when the ad reports itself as completed. If you closed it early, or it failed midway, nothing is granted. If an ad played all the way through and you still got nothing, email us — that one we want to hear about.

Can I turn ads off?

Not inside the game — they are what makes it free. Ads never interrupt a run: one may play between runs, and the other two are buttons you choose to tap. Playing offline stops them loading entirely and the game still works.

How do I change my ad consent choice?

Pause → Ad Privacy Options. The button only appears in regions where the choice applies.

Did my scores sync anywhere?

No. Your best score, best wave and total kills live on your device only. Uninstalling deletes them, and we have no copy to restore.

The game runs slowly on my phone

Turn screen shake down or off in the pause menu, and close background apps. Tell us your device model if it is still rough — we would rather fix it than have you uninstall.
